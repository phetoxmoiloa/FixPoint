ABOUT FIXPOINT

Repairs with
straight answers.

FixPoint is a fictional South African repair service built around one idea: people deserve to understand what is wrong with their device.

OUR STORY

Why FixPoint
exists.

Founded in 2024 (fictional) in Pretoria by a small team of technicians frustrated by vague quotes and jargon. FixPoint began with a workbench and a promise: explain first, estimate second, repair third.

01

Mission

To make device repair clear, fair and accessible for everyday South Africans.

02

Vision

A country where no one replaces a repairable device because they were confused or overcharged.

03

Values

Honest estimates, careful handling, plain language and respect for customer data.
