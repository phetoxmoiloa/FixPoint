# Project Notes

## Concept
FixPoint is a fictional device repair and support service. Its purpose is to give users a simple path from identifying a device problem to requesting an assessment.

## Intended audience
People who use smartphones, laptops and tablets and need help understanding a device fault or the next step in getting it repaired.

## User needs addressed
1. Find out whether the service supports their device.
2. Understand the types of issues covered.
3. See example assessment prices.
4. Describe a device problem in a structured form.
5. Find contact and business-hour information.

## Page purpose
- **Home:** Introduces the service and directs visitors to services or a booking request.
- **Services:** Explains the four broad service categories.
- **Pricing:** Shows sample prices and clarifies that final costs depend on assessment.
- **Book a repair:** Collects the information a repair provider would need for an initial enquiry.
- **Contact:** Provides contact channels and a general enquiry form.

## Content and asset notes
The visual treatment uses CSS-created shapes and interface elements rather than copied brand photography. All business details and prices are fictional. The example email uses the reserved `.example` domain and is not a live contact address.

## Functional scope
JavaScript handles the responsive navigation state and client-side form validation. No personal information is transmitted or stored.
